# Content policy
Clear underlying work and exact edition/material rights separately, with sources and reviewer decisions. Archive access does not imply republication permission. Link rather than mirror uncertain material. Do not reuse a modern introduction, translation, image, annotation, or cover without its own rights basis.

Preserve spelling, verse lines, stanzas, punctuation, and attribution. Record corrections separately. OCR is draft, never automatically published. Every authentic line must be manually compared with a lawful source. Require approved verification and completed proofreading before publication.

The validator blocks non-demo books lacking cleared work and edition status, separate work/edition evidence, dated reviewer decisions, approved proofreading, or source/edition identity. Reviewer authenticity and evidence quality require human review; a metadata flag alone does not prove legal rights. Production-mode validation excludes all demo books. The current app is a visibly labeled demo pilot.

Before launch, configure a correction/takedown contact. Reports should name the book/chapter/block, source, and requested remedy. Maintain a transparent correction history and review claims promptly.

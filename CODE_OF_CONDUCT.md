# Community code of conduct
Treat readers and contributors with respect across languages, identities, abilities, and levels of experience. Offer constructive criticism; credit sources and labor. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful contributions or restrict participation. Handle reports privately and fairly, protect reporter privacy, and explain moderation decisions where appropriate. A confidential maintainer reporting address must be configured before accepting public community contributions; do not publish sensitive reports in public issues.

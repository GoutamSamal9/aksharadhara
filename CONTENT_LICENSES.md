# Literary content rights
The MIT application license does not cover historical literary works, editions, scans, introductions, illustrations, or translations.

`content/books/demo-garden.json`: original interface demonstration created for Akshara; released under CC0-1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). It is not a historical transcription or an attributed poet's work. The demo exists only in the labeled pilot, and is rejected by the authentic production validator.

No authentic literary works are included. Each future work must carry rights evidence for both work and edition/material, source metadata, and an approved proofreading decision. See docs/CONTENT_POLICY.md.
